GSoC Proposal · ros2_control

Physical AI Inference → ros2_control Bridge

A proposed Inference-to-Control Bridge inside ros2_control. AI motion policies output joint targets at only 20–50 Hz, far too coarse to drive motors directly.

The bridge buffers upcoming targets and upsamples them into smooth joint commands at up to 2 kHz using quintic spline / minimum-jerk interpolation, with real-time jerk-limit monitors guarding the hardware.
